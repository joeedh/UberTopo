## 1. Goals

1. **Dropped slots stay dead.** A slot dropped by compaction, freeze or a region rebuild
   never revives an old handle when it is re-allocated (closes the DEC-014 limit and
   C13's shell/region gap).
   - **Not covered:** journaled history. Rollback and undo restore generations exactly
     (that is what makes them exact), so a handle taken inside undone work can resolve
     again once its slot is reused. Such handles must be re-checked by persistent ID.
     Pinned by a test.
2. **Compaction while editing**, as a journaled barrier.
3. **Region identity that survives edits** (the cause of OI-P3-1, review #8): a hinted
   region's ID comes from its hint alone, so splitting its faces doesn't rename it and
   rebuilding doesn't drop its attributes (DEC-017).
4. **A merged room keeps its sensors** (OI-P3-2).
5. **The remaining primitives a consumer needs now:**
   - `imprint` / `unimprint` (P4 imprints roads on terrain sheets);
   - `add_hole` / `remove_hole`;
   - `weld` (vertex pairs, fusing the parallel edges that result).
6. **Per-primitive cost independent of model size** in `Local` mode, for successful and
   failing primitives, excluding barriers (`rebuild_regions`, `compact`) and the undo
   of barriers. Region-containment checks of touched elements cost O(region boundary)
   and are the stated exception.
7. **Validator gap closed:** wires crossing faces are reported.

**Cut or deferred (with reasons in §10b):**
- incremental region maintenance / `relabel_regions` (OI-P3-1 stays open, narrowed);
- whole-group journal captures;
- `split_vertex`, `merge_edges` as separate primitives, `connect_loops` /
  `disconnect_loops`;
- the exact integer remainder rule for split-by-measure;
- dirty tiles.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Generation floor (W1)

- **State.** Each `Domain` gains `gen_floor_` (u32, default 0). Invariant: no handle
  with `gen ≥ gen_floor_` was ever issued for an index at or beyond the current
  capacity.
- **`permute(map, new_size)`** raises the floor over old indices `≥ new_size` only:
  `floor = max(floor, gen + 1)` for each. In-range moves (freeze's Morton order) keep
  freeze's existing `max(own, slot's old) + 1` rule and don't touch the floor, so the
  P2 pipeline leaves every floor at 0.
- **Replacing a derived domain** (`build_regions` writes new shells and regions):
  - a new row at index `i < old capacity` gets `gen = max(old gen[i] + 1, floor)`;
  - a new row beyond the old capacity gets `gen = floor`;
  - old rows at indices `≥ new size` raise the floor as `permute` does;
  - an empty old domain leaves everything at 0, so a first `build_regions` is unchanged.
- **Growth.** A slot appended past capacity (`alloc` with no free slot, `alloc_n`)
  starts at `gen = floor`. Freed-and-reused slots keep the `+1` rule.
- **Not raised by** the editor's rollback resize. Floors are part of the captured
  state: `Savepoint` and `TxnLog` record them and `restore` puts them back.
- **Serialised.** UTCX format 2 writes seven floors after the header (one per domain;
  face sides follow faces; the global group has no allocator). Format 1 still reads,
  with floors 0.
- **Hashed** in `content_hash` and `topology_hash` only when one is non-zero, after a
  marker byte. All-zero states hash exactly as before.

### 3.2 Compaction (W2)

- **`compact(k)`** (`topo/compact.h/.cc`) drops tombstones in every domain, keeping
  live slots in index order (faces and side rows together).
  - Factors `identity_map`, `gens_of` and `new_generations` out of `freeze.cc`; freeze
    calls the shared code.
  - Rewrites stored indices with `remap_group` (`remap.h`).
  - Asserts that no container names a dropped element.
  - Returns the per-domain maps.
- **`Editor::compact()`** is a barrier transaction like `rebuild_regions` (P3a's
  whole-complex snapshot), records an op, and invalidates the editor's indices.
  Redo re-runs it (it is deterministic).

### 3.3 Regions (W3, DEC-017)

**Stable hinted IDs.**
- A region whose winning hint `h` is won by no other region gets
  `region_id_from_hint(h)`, a new derivation (`derive(tag::Region, "hint", h)`).
- Regions that share a winning hint keep `region_id(h, min side ID)` and the
  `DuplicateHint` warning, as now.
- Unhinted regions keep `region_id_from_sides(min side ID)`: they are anonymous, and a
  split that changes their minimum side renames them. Their carried-over attributes are
  lost then. Documented as a known limit.
- **Golden impact.** The P2 determinism golden and the P3a random golden change (hinted
  room IDs). Both are re-pinned with DEC-017 as the reason, after checking that only
  region IDs, the region row order they sort, and `.f.region` values move.
- **Vote stability.** Votes still count sides. A split adds a side carrying the
  parent's hint, which can break a tie. Documented, tested.

**Container hints (OI-P3-2).** A container hint resolves:
1. to the region whose winning hint it is, if exactly one (the P2 rule, unchanged);
2. otherwise to the unique region whose sides carry it as a minority hint;
3. otherwise it is `UnresolvedContainerHint` (no carrier) or `AmbiguousContainerHint`.

Every case P2 resolved resolves the same way. Only cases P2 reported as unresolved now
resolve, such as a sensor whose room was merged into another by `kill_face`.

**OI-P3-1 (narrowed).** Face edits still set `regions_dirty`. With stable hinted IDs,
a fast path would still have to re-sort shells (ordered by minimum side ID) and rename
unhinted regions. That makes it O(F) in the worst case and its equivalence to the
oracle subtle (review #6, #10, #13). It waits for a measured need.

### 3.4 Editor indices (W4)

- **ID index** (vertices, edges, faces): a sorted map with check-on-insert. A duplicate
  found while building or inserting is reported (`DuplicateId` from the primitive, or
  an index error the validator surfaces). Loop IDs are unique by construction
  (face ID + ordinal) and aren't indexed.
- **Inclusion index:** `container → sorted elements`, for face containers only. Region
  containers are resolved from hints by every rebuild, so they are read from the
  container columns when needed (only `embed_impl`'s region branch and `kill_face`'s
  checks need them).
- **Hint index:**
  - counts of side carriers per hint (`hint_carried`);
  - counts of container users per hint (`hint_in_use`);
  - a per-region set of carried hints, rebuilt by `rebuild_regions`, for `embed_impl`'s
    region branch.
- **Maintenance.** Every write of a container, hint or ID goes through one helper per
  column (`set_container`, `set_hint`, `set_id`), including `free` resets and
  `flip_face`'s hint swap.
- **Rollback re-indexes instead of invalidating.** `restore` removes the index entries
  of each row it overwrites or drops and adds those of the restored bytes. Undo and redo
  do the same through the same path. Only outside writes (version mismatch) and
  barriers invalidate an index.
- **Free set.** `Domain`'s min-heap becomes a structure with O(log n) insert, erase and
  min (`undo_free` is linear today).

### 3.5 Filtered validation and OnCommit (W5)

- **Combinatorial layer.** `validate_local` checks touched rows and their one-ring:
  - disk cycles at touched vertices;
  - radial cycles of touched edges;
  - loops and faces of touched coedges, loops and faces;
  - ID uniqueness of touched IDs through the editor's index.
  The full layer stays the oracle (`Full` mode, and the random test's equivalence run).
- **Embedding layer.** Iterate the filter lists, never capacity. Region containers need
  the region's boundary fan only for touched region-contained elements.
- **Global layer.** The editor keeps a **face-box grid** (uniform, over the frame's
  quanta) updated for touched faces. The filtered contact check queries it instead of
  building boxes and a grid for every face. Wires are in the grid too (W8).
- **`ValidateMode::OnCommit`.** Primitives don't validate. The touched set accumulates
  across the transaction's primitives (rows freed and re-used stay in it). The
  outermost commit validates it once (Local), or Full if the transaction contains a
  barrier. A failure rolls back the whole transaction.
- **Redo never validates.** It re-executes a committed, validated transaction on the
  same state (the journal's staleness check guarantees that). This also lets
  OnCommit transactions whose intermediate states are invalid be redone.

### 3.6 Primitives (W6, W7)

**Common rules.** As P3a: savepoint per primitive, `Prim`, propagation events, lineage,
refusal codes as bit-identical no-ops, ID uniqueness, `regions_dirty` on any face edit.
On a projected face, the face's edges are re-sorted after the edit and
`fix_projection` runs (as `split_face` does).

**`imprint(f, chain)`** — `chain` lists wire edges embedded in `f`, in path order.
- **Shared requirements:**
  - chain edges are distinct and form a simple path or cycle;
  - every interior chain vertex is embedded in `f` and has exactly its two chain edges;
  - chain elements are left out of the inclusion reassignment.
- **Split case:** the chain runs from `a` to `b`, two distinct vertices of `f`'s outer
  loop, and touches no loop elsewhere.
  - Effect: `split_face` with the chain as the chord. Chain edges lose their container
    and gain two coedges each; interior vertices lose their container.
  - **IDs as `split_face`** (design 04 §3): children
    `derive(OpDerived, f.id, "split_face", 0 | 1)`; chain edge IDs unchanged. The child
    holding the chain coedges `a → b` keeps `f`'s slot.
  - **Shared core.** `split_face` and `imprint` share `split_face_core(f, path, ids)`.
    The `Adjacent` and `EdgeExists` checks belong to `split_face` only. `split_face`'s
    allocation order and behaviour are unchanged.
- **Island case:** the chain is a closed cycle whose vertices are all embedded in `f`.
  - Effect: the cycle becomes a new hole loop of `f` (appended, ID
    `loop_id(f.id, ordinal)`) and the outer loop of a new face `g`, whose front faces
    the same way as `f`'s. Inclusions inside the cycle move to `g`. Hints, flags and
    surface copy from `f`.
  - `f` keeps its ID; `g = derive(OpDerived, f.id, "imprint.island", min chain edge
    ID)`.
  - Refused if one of `f`'s holes lies inside the cycle (`HoleInside`): nested holes
    are rejected by the CDT, and moving holes to `g` is deferred.
- **Refused:** an endpoint on a hole loop or on two loops (`NotOuterLoop`), a chain
  vertex with other edges or touching a loop at an interior vertex (`ChainNotSimple`),
  a chain edge not embedded in `f` (`NotEmbedded`), an inclusion on the chain
  (`InclusionOnChord`), a degenerate child (`DegenerateLoop`).

**`unimprint(e)`** — walks the maximal chain through `e` whose edges each have exactly
two coedges, on faces `f` and `g`, and whose interior vertices have degree 2.
- **Requirements:** those of `join_faces` (coplanar, equal flags, surface and hints,
  consistent fronts). In the split case, `f` and `g` share exactly the chain
  (otherwise `NotJoinable`).
- **Split case:** survivor = the smaller face ID, as `join_faces`. The chain's edges and
  interior vertices become embedded in the survivor.
- **Island case:** the chain is `g`'s whole outer loop and one of `f`'s holes, and `g`
  has no holes (`HoleInside`). `g` is freed, the hole loop removed (later holes
  renumbered), and the chain and `g`'s inclusions are embedded in `f`.

**`add_hole(f, chain)`** — a closed simple chain of wires embedded in `f`, every vertex
of degree 2.
- Strictly inside the outer loop and outside the other holes (exact point tests on every
  chain vertex). Crossings are left to validation.
- Refused if one of `f`'s inclusions lies inside the cycle (`ContainedElement`), or one
  of `f`'s holes does (`HoleInside`).
- The hole is appended with ID `loop_id(f.id, ordinal)`.

**`remove_hole(f, loop)`** — the hole's edges carry no other coedges (`EdgeShared`) and
its vertices have no other face edges (`EdgeShared`). They become embedded in `f`;
later holes are renumbered (lineage records the renames).

**`weld(pairs)`** — replaces v1's `merge_vertices` + `merge_edges` (review #1, #2).
- **Arguments:** a list of vertex pairs `(a, b)`.
  - Each pair coincides exactly (`Coincident` otherwise).
  - The pairs are disjoint.
  - No pair is joined by an edge (`Adjacent`).
- **Effect, in one primitive:**
  1. Each pair merges into the vertex with the smaller ID; disk cycles concatenate,
     survivor first.
  2. Edges that now have the same endpoint set are fused: survivor = the smaller edge
     ID. The others' coedges move to it, with the sense toggled where the `vs` order
     differs, and the radial cycle is re-sorted (`RadialTie` fails: coincident faces).
     Absorbed edges leave the disk cycles and are freed.
- **Containers:**
  - equal containers are kept;
  - a vertex or wire that ends up with coedges loses its container;
  - otherwise `Incompatible`.
- **Orientation.** Edges keep their `vs` order. The convention "edges run from the
  smaller vertex ID" is the builder's and `make_edge`'s, and the validator doesn't
  require it.
- **Regions:** dirty if any coedge moved between edges. Welding only wires and isolated
  vertices keeps regions.
- **Propagation:** `Merge` with equal weights for vertices and edges. Lineage records
  `Merge`.
- **Typical use:**
  - welding a wall onto another body: kill the duplicate wall, then weld its rim's
    vertex pairs in one call;
  - snapping a wire end onto a pole: one pair.

### 3.7 Wire–face crossings (W8)

- **Rule.** A wire may meet a face only:
  - at a vertex of the face;
  - at a wire endpoint embedded in that face (a pole on terrain);
  - along its length if the wire is embedded in that face. It must then lie inside the
    face: both endpoints inside or on the boundary, and no proper crossing with any of
    the face's loop edges (exact segment–segment tests in the face's projection).
- **Other contacts** are exact segment–triangle tests against `T(f)`, over the contact
  grid with wires added. New code `WireCrossesFace`.
- **Coverage.** Runs on every P2 generator family to confirm no existing model is
  newly rejected.

### 3.8 Determinism

- All new primitives are deterministic functions of state and arguments. Chain walks
  start at the given edge and follow the given order; welds choose survivors by ID and
  process pairs in ascending survivor ID.
- Index maintenance never changes results, only their cost; the random test's
  Local/Full equivalence run checks this.

## 5. Risks

- **R1 — Golden churn.** The P2 and P3a goldens change once, for DEC-017 and the floor
  rule. Before re-pinning, a diff of the region columns must show that nothing else
  moved.
- **R2 — Re-indexing on rollback** is a second write path. It is covered by mutation 9
  and by the random test's Local/Full equivalence, which uses the indices.
- **R3 — Scope.** W5 (the face-box grid and filtered layers) is the largest item. If it
  slips, Goal 6 is reported as measured, not met.

## 6. Open issues

- **OI-P3-1 (narrowed, §3.3).** Incremental regions after face splits wait for a
  measured need.
  - **Measured 2026-11-22** (`bench_regions`, clang-cl Release; runs vary up to 2×):

  - At 10⁵ faces, region building is 200 to 2000 times the edit that dirtied the
    regions. Most of the barrier isn't `build_regions` (about 175 ms when timed
    inside it):
    - flushing the editor's index: about 185 ms;
    - rebuilding the face-box grid: about 115 ms;
    - in `Local` mode, a full `validate(Embedding)`: about 520 ms.

    `build_regions` changes no geometry, so the grid rebuild and the embedding
    validation are candidates to skip, which may cut the barrier by a half or
    more before any incremental scheme. Why the barrier does them hasn't been
    checked yet (C13).
  - The road-on-terrain case (1663 faces, 140 curved edges): `build_regions`
    12–22 ms.
- **OI-P3b-1.** Island imprint and `add_hole` refuse cycles that enclose existing holes.
- **OI-P3b-2.** Unhinted regions are renamed by splits that change their minimum side,
  which drops their carried-over attributes.

- vertices: ID and position, sorted by ID;
- edges: ID and the sorted IDs of their endpoints;
- faces: ID, flags, hints, and each loop as its vertex-ID cycle rotated to the
  smallest ID;
- when regions are clean: the region ID of each face side.

No generations, `.live`, row order, `.v.e`, `.l.c`, or disk or radial order. Edge IDs
are left out of the comparison, since the fused edge keeps the smaller of two IDs that
the builder can't be told to produce. Edges are compared by their endpoint IDs.

**C3 — Welding recipe.** Body B is built *open*: no duplicate wall, with rim vertices
coincident with A's wall corners. One `weld` of the four pairs then goes from an invalid
state to a valid one. §3.6's "typical use" changes to match. Killing the duplicate wall
first is not offered: the kill itself is refused while the bodies overlap.

**C4 — Container hints.**
1. Exactly one region wins the hint → that region (P2).
2. Two or more regions win it → `AmbiguousContainerHint` (P2).
3. No region wins it → the unique region other than region 0 that carries it as a
   minority hint. Two or more carriers → `Ambiguous`; none → `Unresolved`.

Region 0's sides don't count as carriers, because P2 drops region 0's votes.
`embed_impl` writes the region's winning hint. A test covers the duplicate-winner case.

**C5 — Wire–face rule, corrected.**
- **Vertex contact.** A wire may touch a face at a point only:
  - at a vertex the wire and the face share (a wire endpoint);
  - or at a wire endpoint embedded in that face.
- **A wire embedded in `f`:** split the segment at every point where it touches `f`'s
  boundary. Each piece's midpoint must be inside `f`, by an exact point-in-polygon test
  in the face's projection on doubled coordinates.
- **Tests:** a wire across a hole, a wire through a reflex corner, and a wire through a
  face corner mid-length must each be refused.

**C6 — Grid rollback.**
- **Recording.** Each savepoint records the faces whose boxes it changed: touched
  faces, plus the faces at touched vertices.
- **Restoring.** After `restore` completes, their boxes are recomputed, and the boxes
  of freed or dropped faces are removed. Undo and redo do the same.
- **Mutation:** the grid is not refreshed on rollback.

**C7 — Large faces.**
- **Cell size:** a power of two in quanta, the smallest one ≥ the median face box
  extent at grid build. It stays fixed until the next barrier, which rebuilds the grid.
- **Large faces** (spanning more than 64 cells) are gridded by their `T(f)` triangles
  instead of their box. Contact tests then touch only the nearby triangles.
- **Goal 6** holds for edits whose neighbouring faces have bounded triangle counts.
  - **Exception:** an edit *of* a large face, such as imprinting a road on terrain,
    costs O(that face's triangles), because `T(f)` is re-derived.
- **Benchmark:** a terrain-plus-roads case is added, reported but not gated.

**C8 — DEC-017 wording.**
- **Stable:** a region carrying a single hint (a room) keeps its ID under splits.
- **Not stable:**
  - mixed-hint regions can flip their winner on any change in side counts, and are
    then renamed;
  - a split that makes a hint a duplicate winner fails container resolution until
    resolved.
- **Re-pin diff list:** `.id` and the order of regions, `.f.region`, `.s.region`,
  `.r.shell`, `.r.flags`, the region indices in `.v.container` / `.e.container`, and
  the generations of reordered rows. Nothing else may differ.

**C9 — Local combinatorial scope.** Each combinatorial `ValidateCode` gets an explicit
local scope, listed in the implementation notes. At minimum:
- **VertexLink:** at touched vertices, and at both ends of edges whose radial cycles
  changed (P3 C14);
- **`ContainerOnFaceVertex` and `ContainerInvalid`:** on touched vertices and edges;
- **`EdgeCoedge` (`.e.c`) and `VertexEdge` (`.v.e`):** on touched edges and vertices;
- **invariant W:** on touched coedges when regions are clean.

**C10 — Re-indexing order.**
- `restore` first removes the index entries of appended rows, then replays the
  captures, removing and re-adding the entries of each row.
- After any rollback, undo or redo that re-indexes, `ids_stamp_` is re-synced to the
  new version.

**C11 — Redo check.** Each `Txn` stores its post-commit content hash.
- With `EditorOptions::verify_redo` set, redo compares against it and fails with
  `JournalStale` on a mismatch.
- The option is on by default in debug builds and off in release, because a content
  hash costs O(N).

**C12 — Equivalence scope.** Local, Full and OnCommit hashes are compared on runs of
one-primitive transactions. Runs with multi-primitive OnCommit transactions instead
check that every commit passes Full validation.

**C13 — Remaining O(F) costs.**
- Barriers rebuild the indices and the grid eagerly, inside the barrier.
- Filtered validators use sparse per-call storage (maps keyed by touched rows), never
  F-sized arrays.
- **Region-0 containment** costs one ray cast over the grid: O(faces the ray meets),
  not O(region boundary).
- **`kill_face` and `embed_impl`** read region containers from the hint index (§3.4),
  never by a column scan.

**C14 — Spec gaps.**
- **`add_hole`:** the chain's edges and vertices lose their containers. The new
  coedges set `.e.c` by the minimum-face-ID rule, through `link_radial`.
- **`weld`:** for each absorbed vertex `b`, every `.e.vs` entry and every `.c.v` that
  names `b` is rewritten to the survivor. The coedges are found through `b`'s disk
  cycle.
- **`weld` containers.** Containers are compared as `(container, container_hint)`.
  The result is:
  - none, if the vertex has coedges afterwards;
  - otherwise a face container, if either side has one (two different faces →
    `Incompatible`);
  - otherwise a region container and hint, if either side has one (two different ones
    → `Incompatible`);
  - otherwise none.

  So a region wire's endpoint welded onto a pole base embedded in terrain takes the
  face container.

**C15 — Additional mutations:**
- rule 3 applied with duplicate winners;
- the grid not refreshed on rollback;
- `weld` keeping a face vertex's container;
- VertexLink outside the local scope;
- a wire across a hole accepted;
- `compact` allowed inside a transaction.

**Order.** W5 ran after W6–W8. None of the primitives needed the grid. W8's rule went
into the full layer first, and the filtered layer reuses it
(`vdetail::embedded_wire_ok`).

**Deviations from v3, with reasons:**
- **`replace_generations` keeps unchanged rows.** A region rebuild keeps the generation
  of a shell with the same first side and of a region with the same ID, rather than
  bumping everything, so handles to untouched rooms survive a rebuild.
- **Floors are not recorded in `TxnLog`.** Only barriers change them, and a barrier is
  rolled back or undone by swapping in its snapshot. Mutation 2 ("rollback doesn't
  restore the floor") therefore has nothing to break and was not run.
- **The inclusion index covers region containers too.** C13's hint index is the same
  structure.
- **The C5 rule is implemented without splitting the segment.** A wire embedded in `f`
  must cross no loop edge, overlap none, pass through no corner of `f`, and have its
  exact midpoint strictly inside. With those exclusions the only boundary touches left
  are at the wire's endpoints, so this is the same as splitting at every touch. A wire
  through a reflex corner is refused, as C5's test list requires.
- **`make_edge` gained an `in` argument** (embed on creation). W8 broke tests that
  embedded a wire in a face in two primitives: between them the wire is free and
  crosses the face. See `debugging.md`.
- **The weld test compares structure by position (C2), not by persistent ID.** Body
  B's rim vertices carry their own IDs by construction, so an ID-keyed comparison with
  a directly built pair cannot match. The test compares:
  - vertex positions;
  - edges with their coedge counts;
  - faces as oriented position cycles;
  - region and shell counts.

  Positions are exact in both coordinate types.
- **OnCommit equivalence (C12).** The random step mix now has multi-primitive
  transactions with invalid intermediate states (weld after `set_position`, and the
  setup of holes and imprints). OnCommit runs are therefore not hash-compared; every
  OnCommit commit is checked for full validity instead. `Local` is still hash-equal to
  `Full`.
- **Grid cell size (C7)** is a power of two in world units (doubles), not in frame
  quanta.
- **Local combinatorial scope (C9):**
  - loop IDs are checked among the in-scope loops only, because the index holds vertex,
    edge and face IDs;
  - shell and region structure is checked only by barriers, which validate in full;
  - the full scope is listed in the header of `topo/validate_local.cc`.
- **`verify_redo` for barriers.** On a mismatch the redone barrier is undone and the
  journal cleared (`JournalStale`).
- **Goldens.** `test_edit_random` is pinned for all three seeds. Seeds 2 and 3 end with
  hinted rooms and cover DEC-017; seed 1 ends with region 0 only (the weakness noted
  during W3).
- **litestl.** `util::Map::lookup_ptr` gained a const overload, on branch
  `ubertopo/map-const-lookup` (fc7cb43). Pushed on 2026-11-18, together with litestl
  master (608afca, the P1b work).
